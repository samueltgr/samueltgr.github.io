04

Logo System

Our visual identity relies on two primary elements: the Bee logomark and the Industrious wordmark.

download Download Logo

The Bee, explained:

Bees, belonging to a hive, are often described as “industrious” and represent the power of collaboration and community.

+

Industrious, explained:

To be industrious, is to be diligent and hardworking in nature — contributing meaningfully to an endeavor.

=

Together:

Together, these elements are the perfect embodiment of our inspiring member community, our dedicated employees, and our mission as a company. But don’t let the seriousness of our name fool you — Industrious is buzzing with excitement and we love to have a good time!

Horizontal:

Our most widely used logo format. Suitable for all placements, particularly landscape formats.

Stacked (Small Bee):

Best for portrait or square formats, or instances where the content is centered.

Stacked (Large Bee):

Best for square formats or instances where our bee icon should be more dominant.

Vertical:

Best for tall, narrow placements.

Bee Only:

Our bee icon may be used without the Industrious for internal and member comms. For external comms, the bee icon should not be used alone, unless as a graphic element or approved placement.

Industrious Only:

Industrious should always appear with the Bee, unless a placement doesn't allow it or omission of the bee has been approved.

06

Photography & Media

Two pillars, always shot in natural light: the architecture that makes a room feel considered, and the people who make it feel alive.

Architectural

Space & structure

Spatial design, abundant natural light, bespoke millwork, panoramic views.

  • Natural sunlight over harsh flash
  • Wide room context, paired with tactile close-ups
  • Subtle brand integration within the architecture
Lifestyle

People & hospitality

Authentic member joy, collaborative energy, the white-glove hospitality that sets us apart.

  • Candid connection, no stiff stock poses
  • Hospitality amenities: coffee craft, breakfast, happy hours
  • Motion and energetic productivity, not static setups
